Title: Advanced VPN Troubleshooting
Category: Network Access

For persistent VPN issues that survive a reinstall of the client, engineers should collect a client-side debug log by running the diagnostic tool from the Start Menu under GlobalProtect > Collect Logs, then attach the resulting zip file to the support ticket. Check that the local time zone is set correctly, since certificate validation can fail if the system clock is more than 5 minutes off from the gateway.

If the debug log shows repeated "certificate handshake failed" errors, the client certificate may have expired and should be reissued by the security team through the standard certificate renewal request. Most advanced VPN cases are resolved once the certificate is reissued and the client reinstalled.
