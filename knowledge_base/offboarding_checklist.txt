Title: Employee Offboarding Checklist
Category: Offboarding

When an employee departs, the manager must submit an offboarding request through the IT Service Portal at least 2 business days before the last day, specifying the exact last working day and time. IT disables network, email, and VPN access at end of day on the last working day, and forwards email is set up only if explicitly requested and approved. Company hardware, including laptops, badges, and any peripherals, must be returned to IT or shipped back within 5 business days of departure.
